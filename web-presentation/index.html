Operational Workflow

👁️

Detection

Real-time computer vision scans the aquatic environment for micro-plastics and debris.

🧠

Decision

On-board AI determines the most efficient path to intercept and collect identified waste.

🐟

Movement

Propelled by bio-inspired fin mechanics for silent, efficient, and natural maneuvering.

🧺

Collection

Active intake mechanism captures debris and transfers it to the internal storage hull.

System Architecture

1. Head

Vision system for real-time object detection and spatial mapping.

2. Gill Structure

Intake and filtering mechanism to capture micro-plastics from the water stream.

3. Body

Integrated storage system for collected waste with capacity monitoring.

4. Tail

High-efficiency propulsion system mimicking natural fish locomotion.
